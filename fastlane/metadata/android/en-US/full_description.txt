Minimal Agenda is a minimalist agenda that shows the events from the calendars already on your device, in one clean, distraction-free list.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Agenda view of your upcoming events, plus month and year overviews</li>
<li>Works with every calendar on your device (Google, Nextcloud/DAVx5, local and others)</li>
<li>Add, edit and delete events; recurring events and multi-day events are supported</li>
<li>Optional reminders for individual events, with discreet notifications that hide event names on the lock screen</li>
<li>Light, dark and system themes, accent colors</li>
<li>If your device has no calendar, you can create a device-only one</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>No data collection, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads</li>
<li>No network access: your events never leave your device</li>
<li>Calendar data is read and written only through the Android system calendar</li>
</ul>
