# Privacy Policy

Minimal Agenda collects no data.

- **No collection:** the app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no user accounts, and it
  sends nothing to the developer or to third parties.
- **Calendar data:** the app reads and writes calendar events only on your device, through the Android system
  calendar, and only with the calendar permission you grant. Events are never uploaded by the app.
- **Notifications:** notifications are used only for the event reminders you set. They are scheduled and shown
  locally on your device.
- **Links:** the app opens a link in your browser only when you tap one. The app itself makes no network requests.
- **Local settings:** your preferences (theme, colors, reminders) are stored on your device only. Android app
  backups and device transfers exclude this data, including saved reminder event names.
